## Switching Themes

Follow these steps to change your active dashboard theme:

1. Click the **Palette Icon** (<i className="fa-solid fa-palette"></i>) in the upper-right corner of the top navigation bar.
2. Select your desired theme from the dropdown menu:
   - `Deep Ocean`
   - `Midnight Blue`
   - `Lavender Haze`
   - `Amber Warmth`
3. The interface theme tokens switch immediately without refreshing or interrupting running searches.
4. Your theme selection is automatically persisted to browser `localStorage` and restored on subsequent visits.
